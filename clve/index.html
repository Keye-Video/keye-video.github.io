Closed-Loop Evaluation of Video Understanding 闭环视频理解评测（CLVE）

An evaluator watches the same raw video as the candidate and adapts visual-only follow-up questions across three levels—eliminating timestamp leakage, audio shortcuts, and numeric judge midpoint collapse.

评测器与候选模型同步观看原始视频，并依据前轮回答动态生成三级纯视觉追问，系统性解决时间戳泄露、音频捷径与数值打分器中点坍缩三大问题。

Abstract摘要

Video understanding benchmarks fix their questions and answer options in advance. Such open-loop tests cannot tell whether a model answered from what it saw or from cues in the options, the text, or the audio, and once scores saturate they no longer show where perception fails. We study closed-loop evaluation instead: an evaluator model watches the same raw video as the candidate model and chooses each question based on the previous answers.

A naive version of this setup fails in three ways: the evaluator reveals timestamps in follow-up questions, audio-related questions let omni-modal models compensate for weak vision, and a numeric LLM judge pushes most scores to the midpoint. We present CLVE, a protocol addressing all three issues. Every thread begins with a shared visual question and moves through three escalating levels; threads stop at the first incomplete answer, timestamps are blocked by a rule-based filter, and categorical verdicts are converted to scores afterwards. The evaluator supports both black-box questioning (answers only) and white-box questioning (also seeing uncertain passages in the candidate's reasoning trace), while grading always uses the answers alone.

传统视频理解基准预先固定题目与选项，难以区分模型是真正看到了画面，还是依赖选项、文本或音频捷径，且分数饱和后无法定位感知失效点。我们提出闭环视频理解评测协议 CLVE：评测器与候选模型共同观看原始视频，并基于前轮回答动态发起三级纯视觉追问。针对朴素闭环评测暴露出的时间戳泄露（94%）、音频捷径以及数值打分器中点坍缩三大问题，CLVE 引入纯视觉约束、规则时间戳过滤器与分类裁决机制，并支持黑盒与白盒两种追问模式。

Key Findings核心发现

  1. Closed-loop evaluation widens separation where first-turn scores cluster闭环追问显著拉开模型区分度 On Video-Index 1.1 across six black-box configurations, first-turn scores sit between 63 and 82, whereas CLVE scores span 35.2 to 57.5 (a 22-point spread). Only Qwen 3.8 Flash with tools passes Level 3 on more than 9% of threads (25.5%). 在 Video-Index 1.1 的六组黑盒配置中，首轮得分集中在 63–82，而 CLVE 总分拉开至 35.2–57.5（跨度 22 分）；仅开启工具的 Qwen 3.8 Flash 在 Level 3 通过率超过 9%（达 25.5%）。
  2. Categorical verdicts eliminate midpoint errors at 1/9 the thinking cost分类裁决彻底消除中点偏差，且思考开销降至 1/9 Removing numeric scales from the judge prompt drops midpoint errors from 32%–85% to 0/40 while reducing median thinking tokens from ~7,900 to 882. 去除数值量表、改用“未达标 / 部分达标 / 完全达标”分类裁决后，中点误判降至 0/40，同时单次裁决中位思考 token 从约 7,900 降至 882。
  3. Visual-only rules expose audio shortcuts; white-box questioning probes uncertain reasoning纯视觉规则剔除音频补偿，白盒追问揭示脆弱推理 Excluding audio cues drops Qwen 3.8 Omni 20 points behind Qwen 3.8 Flash (37.6 vs. 57.5). White-box follow-ups targeting uncertain CoT passages lower conditional Level-3 pass rates P(L3|L1) across all three models. 排除音频线索后，Qwen 3.8 Omni 落后 Qwen 3.8 Flash 20 分（37.6 vs. 57.5）；针对推理不确定片段发起白盒质疑后，全部三个模型的条件三级通过率 P(L3|L1) 均下降。
